## Current integrated release sequence

The calendar/document layer is the first eligible near-term activation. Subject to explicit review of the local patch and separate push/deployment approval, it can expose **only the existing stored approved senior calendar and direct-document metadata**. The website can then immediately show those read-only records with source and freshness information.

Draw graphs, active player/pair profiles, rankings, forecasts, simulations, analyst conclusions, and accuracy measures cannot safely be filled from calendar metadata alone. Their activation is conditional on the data and source decisions in the table. No current code is authorised to hide any absence with junior/U-age/Para rows, unapproved senior categories, old stale live records, inferred participant links, fabricated scores, or uncited generated claims.
